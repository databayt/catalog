**A. Make and Let.**
1. Two bank clerks are comparing their bosses. Look at what they say:

Ali: My boss makes me come to work at 7 a.m. every day and he doesn't let me take more than 3 weeks' holiday a year.
Zeinab: Well, my boss lets me have 5 weeks' holiday a year, but she often makes me work on Fridays.

*Look at the words underlined. What is the difference in meaning between 'let' and 'make'? What do you notice about the words that come after 'make' and 'let'?*

2. *Sayed is talking about himself, his family and his friend Imam. Read what he says carefully then fill in the gaps with the correct form of make or let and another appropriate verb. The first one has been done for you.*

a. My father lets me watch TV because I never neglect my lessons.
b. My cousins's parents are not strict with her, they always \_\_\_\_ her \_\_\_ what she wants.
c. My friend Imam is very kind and \_\_\_\_ me \_\_\_ his car every Friday.
d. The film was so sad that it \_\_\_ me \_\_\_.
e. I don't like reading detective stories late at night. They always \_\_\_ me \_\_\_ frightened.
f. When I am sick, my mother \_\_\_ me \_\_\_\_ sleep in my parents' room because it is cooler.
g. When I need help, my eldest brother always says, '\_\_\_ me \_\_\_ you.'
