### SECTION 4: SPINE CHALLENGE

'My new neighbour bangs on the wall at all hours.' said the young man to his friend. 'Doesn't that keep you awake at night?' the friend asked. 'No, but it does interfere with my trumpet practice,' he replied.

A Sudanese man lost his friend in the streets of London. He asked a policeman,'have you seen a Sudanese man pass by?'
'What does he look like,' asked the policeman?
'He's green and....,' said the Sudanese man.
'Oh, don't worry,' said the policeman.' 'If he's green we'll soon find him.'
