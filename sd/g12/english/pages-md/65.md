29th July

Dear David,
How are you? I haven't heard from you for ages. We're fine.
I'm sitting here by the fire, writing to you. Jim is here, too. He's ironing some shirts. My two sons are sleeping. Grandfather is staying with us these days. At the moment he's fast asleep and snoring loudly.

Anyway I have to go now. I want to see the 'World of Sports' programme at 10 p.m. It is going to start in ten minutes. Write and tell us what you are doing.

Steve
