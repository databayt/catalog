What makes the hands of a clock or a watch shine in the dark?

![figure](pages/162.webp)

In a small way, nuclear energy has been used in the home for many years. An alarm clock or a watch which has hands and numbers that can be seen in the dark are using nuclear power to light up the hand sand numbers. If you have ever used such a watch, you have used nuclear power!

Although there are now many ways of using nuclear power, the power we get from atoms will be used even more in the years to come. There is a great future for all of mankind if we learn to use nuclear power properly.
