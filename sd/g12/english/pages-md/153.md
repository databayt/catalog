HOUSE RULES

1. You can use the bath but you must clean it afterwords .
2. You must clean your own room and sweep the yard every day.
3. Although there is running water, do not use too much of it because the tank will run dry.
4. You must get up every day at 4 a.m., however, on Fridays you can stay in bed until 4.30 a.m.
5. If you have guests, you can take them to the garden or you can use the sitting room on the first floor.
6. You must be back by 9 p.m. unless you are visiting relatives.
