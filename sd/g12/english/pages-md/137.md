A visitor to America writes about a frightening but funny event.

Last summer I visited my friends John and Kate Harris in the USA. One day John took us in his car to see the beautiful countryside around their home. We drove through a flat valley with high hills on either side. I was enjoying sitting in the back of the car looking at the hills.

After a while a strong wind blew up. The trees were moving backwards and forwards and there was dust everywhere. The road was now right next to the side of the mountains. Suddenly I noticed a large grey rock tumbling down the side of the mountain. It was heading straight for our car. John was talking a lot as he was driving, telling us about the history of the area. I thought he had not noticed the rock, so I hit him on the shoulder and screamed 'Stop the car! That rock's going to hit us!'

John and Kate looked at me in amazement, then laughed. They explained that the 'rock' was in fact a large soft plant in the shape of a ball. John and Kate knew it was a plant, but I thought it was a rock. As we approached, the 'rock' simply bounced over the car.
