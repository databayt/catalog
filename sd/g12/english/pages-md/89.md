We are present in the body in very small quantities, but you cannot live without us. If some of us are not present, you will develop serious illnesses. See how useful we are..........

B helps brain tissue to develop and also helps digestion.

C helps the body build cells used in healing cuts and bruises. It also makes strong teeth and bones.

D again helps teeth and bones.

E keeps the red blood cells healthy.
