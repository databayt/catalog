**Letter Two**

P.O. Box 23099
Kassala

December 19 1996

Dear John,
It was great to hear from you. I'm glad you are enjoying your new job in Port Sudan. I was sorry to hear that your brother had had an accident. I hope he is out of hospital now and that his car was not too badly damaged.

Next week I go for an interview with Kassala Tractor Company. I'm really nervous about it. If I don't get the job, I'll try to come and visit you in Port Sudan.

I'm sending you the photos I took of you at Michael's wedding. They're really good. Write soon and let me know if you've found a better house yet.

Best wishes,

Mohammed
