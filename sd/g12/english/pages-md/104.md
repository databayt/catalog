**Letter Two**

P.O. Box 59
Ed Dueim

March 2 1997

Dear Selwa,
I've now arrived home after a really great visit with you and your family. The journey back by bus was fairly comfortable. My father was at the ferry to meet me and take me home.

I have not started work yet as our house is being painted and we're doing a lot of cleaning. I've looked everywhere for my camera, but I must have left it at your house.

Let me know when you can come and visit me in Ed Dueim. I can take another holiday in two months' time.

Give my regards to your family.

Best wishes,

Ibtisam
