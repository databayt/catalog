Soon he became famous not only as a translator, but also as a journalist. His work was so good that many foreign newspapers and magazines recognised him as a well-informed and talented correspondent. He published his own newspaper 'Al Saraha' which was sold all over the country. His articles were not only informative, but also entertaining.

Abdalla Ragab, then was a very famous and successful man. It may surprise you to know, therefore, that he never had any formal education. He was self-taught! He succeeded because he had a strong will and desire to gain by himself the knowledge and skills he needed.

Before he died he wrote down all his experiences in a book which is called 'The Diary of an Ordinary Man' ('Muthakirat Aghbash').
