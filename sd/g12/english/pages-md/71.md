What is this paragraph about?

Let us begin with the past. The traditional image of women is of women being dependent on men, staying at home all the time waiting for help and support. This idea that women are dependent and unproductive is not really true. Such an idea will delay any serious efforts to end hunger and reduce poverty. Years of experience have shown that poverty does not disappear through welfare and charity but through economic activities carried out by both men and women in all situations. We need to see women as they are: active, effective participants in development.
