-man (e.g. 'spokesman') were once used to refer to male or female. The pronouns 'he' and 'him' were used in the same way.

For example:

1. Whoever loses <u>his</u> passport will be fined.
2. <u>Man</u> can do many things which animals cannot do. His interests are not only life ,food etc. but also beliefs and values.
3. There is a police<u>man</u>'s post advertised today.
4. They chose a spokes<u>man</u> for their committee.

<u>But</u>, now English speaking people use **neutral** words to replace the masculine words.

For example:

1. Whoever loses <u>a</u> passport .will be fined
2. <u>Human beings</u> can do ............. Their interests are not only...........
3. They chose a <u>spokesperson</u> for their committee .
4. There is a <u>police officer's</u> post...............
