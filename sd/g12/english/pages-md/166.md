What is biogas?

In many developing countries animal dung is not wasted, but can be used to provide a cheap source of energy. If animal dung or plant waste is stored in an airtight container, germs attack it and break it down producing gas. This is called biogas. The gas is collected and can be used for cooking, heating water and lighting.
