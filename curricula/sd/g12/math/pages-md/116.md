$\overline{س}$ = ٣٢٫٥ + $\frac{١٦٥}{١٥٠}$

∴ $\overline{س}$ – و = $\frac{١٦٥}{١٥٠}$

∴ $ع^٢$ = $\frac{Σ\ ك\ ح^٢}{Σ\ ك}$ – ( $\overline{س}$ – و )$^٢$

= $\frac{٦١٧٥}{١٥٠}$ – $\left(\frac{١٦٥}{١٥٠}\right)^٢$

= ٤١٫١٧ – ١٫٢١ = ٣٩٫٩٦

∴ ع = $\sqrt{٣٩٫٩٦}$ ≃ ٦٫٣
